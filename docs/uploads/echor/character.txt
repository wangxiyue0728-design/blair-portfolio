Character Narrative: Layla – The Bereaved Daughter
Layla lost her mother a year ago. When she first uses Echor, she selects a memory of her mom cooking dinner. She feels comfort hearing her mother’s voice, smelling the familiar spices, seeing her smile again.
But over time, something feels… off. Her mom’s tone is softer than she remembered. There are extra details — words of reassurance that Layla doesn’t recall being spoken. One day, she notices a memory of her mother singing to her, even though she knows it never happened.
She realizes the system’s AI has filled in gaps and “smoothed” the memory, making it more comforting but less authentic.
What Layla Learns (Step 5 insights through her)
	•	At the personal level: Layla learns that memory replay isn’t just about remembering — it can become a simulation, blending truth and fabrication.
	•	At the social level: If memories can be subtly altered, what happens when people rely on them for therapy, relationships, or justice?
	•	At the design level: She discovers the system needs transparency — users must know when a memory is raw versus AI-modified.
	•	At the future level: Layla questions whether the ability to perfect memories is a gift of healing or a dangerous rewriting of history.
